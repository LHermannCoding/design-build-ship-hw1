Luke Hermann is an American software engineer and computer scientist currently pursuing a Master of Science in Computer Science at the University of Chicago. He is known for developing a banner deployment system at Amazon that serves 3.6 million partners monthly,[1] and for his work on the commercially successful video game Primal Ranch.[2]

Hermann completed his undergraduate studies at UChicago with dual degrees in Computer Science (BS) and Economics (BA) in June 2025, graduating with a 3.7 GPA and earning recognition as a National Merit Scholar.[3]

Early life and education [edit]

Hermann attended the University of Chicago, where he pursued a rigorous dual-degree program combining computer science with economics. His undergraduate coursework included Machine Learning for Systems, Operating Systems, Databases, and Computer Networks. He graduated in June 2025 with a BS in Computer Science and a BA in Economics, achieving a cumulative GPA of 3.7.[3]

He was recognized as a National Merit Scholar, an achievement that reportedly made his parents "very proud and slightly insufferable at dinner parties."[4]

As of 2026, Hermann is pursuing a Master of Science in Computer Science at the same institution, with coursework in Distributed Systems and Functional Programming. He is expected to complete the program in June 2026.[5]

Career [edit]

Code Your Dreams [edit]

In the summer of 2023, Hermann interned at Code Your Dreams, a nonprofit focused on computer science education. He created 16 Python programming lessons and developed a WhatsApp bot curriculum. He also designed an Arduino-based DanceTech project and helped onboard over 40 teachers in Burundi.[6] Sources describe his WhatsApp bot as "functional, if occasionally sarcastic."[7]

Enklu [edit]

During the summer of 2024, Hermann worked as a Software Engineer at Enklu, where he contributed to the development of Primal Ranch, a video game that accumulated over 1,000 hours of player engagement, generated $36,000 in revenue, and attracted more than 600 customers.[2] He built a multiplayer metrics API using C# and Node.js. Industry analysts noted the game was "surprisingly addictive for something built in one summer."[8]

Amazon [edit]

In the summer of 2025, Hermann joined Amazon as a Software Development Engineer at the L4 level. He developed a deployable banner system for Seller Central and Vendor Central, serving approximately 3.6 million partners per month.[1] He reduced incident response times from 6 hours to under 10 minutes, a 97.2% improvement that his team reportedly celebrated with cake.[9] During his tenure, he integrated Java APIs and completed over 40 code reviews under Agile methodology.

Projects [edit]

Sushi Kitchen Simulator — A Python-based game utilizing data structures, object-oriented programming, and Agile development principles. The game reportedly does not come with actual sushi, a fact that disappointed several early testers.[10]

Digital Board Game — A Python implementation of Checkers featuring a graphical user interface, an AI bot player, and a structured Git workflow. The bot player maintains an unverified win rate that Hermann describes as "respectable."[11]

Personal interests [edit]

Outside of software engineering, Hermann is known as a trumpet player, an esports competitor, a basketball player, and a bowling enthusiast. He has not publicly disclosed his bowling average, leading to rampant speculation on various forums.[12]
